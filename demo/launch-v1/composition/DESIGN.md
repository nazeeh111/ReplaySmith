# ReplaySmith launch identity

## Style Prompt
A crisp instrument for browser work: warm paper, charcoal ink, and the studio's copper-orange accent. Large confident proportional type states the product moment; small monospaced labels describe the real capture and controlled fixtures. Actual studio screenshots supply evidence. Transitions push like advancing a workflow, with restrained scale and position motion. Silent landscape, 1920 × 1080, 22 seconds. No invented metrics, implied universal recovery, stock visuals, or mock results.

## Colors
- #f5f4ef: paper canvas, matching studio
- #252825: ink, headlines and branding
- #db542d: primary orange, matching studio
- #777b74: supporting text
- #e3e4dc: tooling borders

## Typography
- System sans-serif: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Segoe UI, Arial, sans-serif
- Monospace: SFMono-Regular, Consolas, Liberation Mono, monospace

## Motion
- Synchronous paused GSAP timeline, registered as replaysmith-launch
- Horizontal push transitions of 0.45 seconds; subtle staggered entrances
- Broad readable holds, no infinite looping or time-dependent animation
- One local renderer worker

## What NOT to Do
- No fabricated success counts, benchmark claims, or fake UI actions
- No neon gradients, decorative dashboards, or unrelated illustrations
- No paid assets, remote fonts, CDN requirements at render time, or narration
- Do not hide outcome limits: page mutations and ambiguity are controlled local fixtures
